# Hero photo prompt (16:9, ~2400x1350 or larger)

Photorealistic wide 16:9 hero image, cinematic. A Saudi professional in a white thobe and red shutter-pattern shemagh, seen from behind at a floor-to-ceiling window in a high-rise studio, looking out over the Riyadh skyline at golden-hour dusk with Kingdom Centre and Al Faisaliah visible. Translucent blue holographic data overlays float over the city. Multi-monitor dashboard desk with charts and a map of Saudi Arabia, deep navy and electric blue (#2F49E0) accents, warm orange sunset. Subject and scene occupy the left 55% of the frame. The right 45% fades smoothly into clean pure white negative space for headline text. No text, no logos, no watermarks.

## Variations
- Woman designer at a multi-monitor desk, same skyline.
- Creative team at a glass whiteboard, Riyadh at dusk behind them.
- Camera crew on a rooftop shooting the skyline at golden hour.

## Usage
Save as public/design/hero4.png, then set `src="/design/hero4.png"` in components/home/Hero.js.
Keep `object-position:left center` (globals.css `.hero2 .bg`), so the subject stays on the left.
